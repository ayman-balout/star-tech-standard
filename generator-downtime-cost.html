Cost of Downtime & Excess Fuel

A fault doesn't just risk a breakdown — it costs real money every day it runs unresolved. Enter what you know below and see the actual Naira figure.

1 Fuel Price

Required — used for the excess fuel calculation below.

2 Excess Fuel Consumption (optional)

If your generator is burning more fuel than it should. Not sure what "normal" looks like for your setup? Run Spec Check first to get your expected L/hr.

3 Downtime Cost (optional)

If the generator is unreliable or out of service. Estimate what an hour without it actually costs you — alternative power source, lost production, spoiled goods, or lost sales.

Not sure what to enter? A rough way to estimate this: what does it cost you (fuel for an alternative source, staff standing idle, orders you couldn't fulfil) for one hour with no power? Even a rough figure makes this calculation useful.
These figures are a direct calculation from the numbers you entered — they are only as accurate as your inputs (fuel price, running hours, and your own downtime cost estimate). This tool doesn't diagnose the fault itself; use Generator DoctorAI or Spec Check for that, then bring the numbers here to see what it's costing you to leave unresolved.
