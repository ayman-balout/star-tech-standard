TANKER OFFLOADING SYSTEM

Every delivery, safely controlled.

Tanker deliveries are handled through a fully controlled offloading skid, ensuring safe and accurate transfer into underground or bulk storage tanks.

  • Camlock coupling — quick-connect fitting between tanker hose and offloading line
  • Y-strainer — filters debris before the pump
  • Transfer pump (duty/standby)
  • High pressure sensor — cutoff protection on the line
  • Level counter — meters volume transferred
  • Control panel — manual on/off operation
  • Overflow switch — stops the pump automatically when the tank is full
  • Static earthing / bonding — safe discharge of static charge during transfer
  • Manual bypass valve — keeps fuel transfer running during pump failure or power outage

DAY TANK DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM

One pump, every day tank, safely metered.

Fuel is transferred from the underground tank to day tanks using a transfer pump with a manual selector panel. Since only one tank is filled at a time, a single flow counter positioned after the pump meters all transfers — no need for a counter on every tank.

  • Transfer pump with manual selector control panel
  • Single flow counter positioned after the pump
  • Each day tank fitted with an overflow switch that automatically stops the pump once full
  • Multiple day tanks filled safely in sequence from one transfer system
