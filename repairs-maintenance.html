OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Generator Maintenance

From routine oil and filter servicing to complex engine overhauls and electrical troubleshooting. We service diesel generators from 13.5 kVA to 2,500 kVA — Perkins, Cummins, and Caterpillar.

01

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled servicing including oil changes, filter replacements, fluid checks, and overall system health inspections.

A structured, manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedule: general maintenance every 200 hours, preventive inspection of the turbocharger and coolant system every 500 hours, a full report and top overhaul at 5,000 hours, and general overhaul up to 10,000 hours depending on engine condition.

02

Fault Diagnosis & Repairs

Rapid troubleshooting for start failures, power supply issues, engine overheating, and control panel alarms.

We cover both engine-side and electrical-side faults, so the real cause is found and fixed — not just the symptom. This includes fuel and injection issues, starting system failures, cooling and overheating faults, and control panel/alarm diagnostics across multiple controller types.

03

Engine & Alternator Overhaul

Major mechanical overhauls, pillow block bearing replacements, drive shaft service, and alternator rewinding.

Alternator winding is our core electrical specialty — rotor and stator rewinding for all capacities up to 2,500 kVA. We also provide electric control panel repair and construction across multiple controller types, plus full generator installation for new or relocated units.
